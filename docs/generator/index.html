02

Colonial Electric: For All Your Generator Needs (Installation, Repair, & Maintenance)

Colonial Electric understands that for homeowners and businesses alike, having a reliable generator is essential for ensuring uninterrupted power supply during outages and emergencies. That’s why Colonial Electric is proud to offer comprehensive services for all your generator needs, including installation, repair, and maintenance.

  1. 01

    Generator Installations

    Our licensed electricians have the expertise to handle the installation of generators of all sizes and types. From assessing your power needs and recommending the right generator for your property to performing the installation with precision and care, we ensure that your backup power system is installed correctly and ready to perform when you need it most.

  2. 02

    Generator Repairs

    When generator malfunction or breakdown happens, our skilled electricians are equipped to diagnose the issue quickly and accurately. We use advanced diagnostic tools and techniques to identify the root cause of the problem and perform the necessary repairs efficiently, restoring your generator to optimal functionality.

  3. 03

    Generator Maintenance

    Regular maintenance is key to ensuring that your generator operates reliably and efficiently year after year. Our maintenance services include thorough inspections, testing of critical components, lubrication, fuel system checks, and other preventive measures to keep your generator in peak condition and ready to spring into action at a moment’s notice

  • Whole Home Generators

    Our Whole Home Generators offer seamless backup power for your entire house during outages. With different sizes and capacities, they integrate directly into your electrical system for automatic operation, ensuring everything runs smoothly when needed.

  • Portable Generators

    Our Portable Generators are compact and versatile, perfect for outdoor events, job sites, and emergencies. Featuring easy-start engines, multiple outlets, and built-in safety features, they offer convenient backup power wherever you go.
