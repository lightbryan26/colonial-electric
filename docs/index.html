07What Should I Do In Case Of An Electrical Emergency?

In the event of an electrical emergency before our Colonial Electric technicians arrive, ensuring your safety is paramount. Here are essential steps to follow:

  1. 01

    Prioritize Safety: Immediately cut off power to the affected area or the entire building if needed. Locate your electrical panel or fuse box and switch off the main breaker.

  2. 02

    Maintain Composure: Stay calm and avoid panic. Clear the vicinity of potential dangers and ensure everyone, including pets, stays a safe distance away.

  3. 03

    Seek Professional Assistance: Reach out to our skilled electricians at Colonial Electric for emergency support. We provide 24/7 service to swiftly address your electrical concerns.

  4. 04

    Avoid DIY Solutions: While awaiting our technicians, refrain from attempting any DIY electrical repairs, as it could be hazardous and exacerbate the problem.

  5. 05

    Provide Clear Information: When contacting us, offer detailed information about the emergency. This aids our technicians in arriving prepared with the necessary tools and equipment.

  6. 06

    Evacuate if Necessary: If the situation poses an immediate threat, such as smoke, sparks, or a burning odor, evacuate the premises and call 911 if needed.

At Colonial Electric, your safety is our utmost priority. Our proficient electricians are adept at handling emergencies and will promptly arrive at your location to assess and resolve the issue, safeguarding you and your property.

08Does My Project Need An Electrical Permit?

Determining whether your project requires an electrical permit is crucial for ensuring compliance with local regulations and safety standards. At Colonial Electric, our team can assist you in navigating this process smoothly.

We understand that the permitting requirements can vary depending on the nature and scope of your project, as well as local building codes. Our knowledgeable electricians are familiar with the permit application process and can provide guidance tailored to your specific needs.

Specifically, if you’re planning to install a new electrical system on your property, upgrade existing wiring, or undertake major renovations involving electrical work, it’s likely that you’ll need to obtain an electrical permit.
