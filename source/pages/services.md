## Electrical Repairs

Electrical repairs involve fixing any issues or malfunctions within an existing electrical system. This could include repairing faulty wiring, addressing power outages, replacing damaged outlets or switches, troubleshooting circuit breakers, or fixing any other electrical components that are not functioning correctly. 

An Electrical Inspection is often necessary to identify these issues and ensure that all repairs are addressed properly.

## Electrical Upgrades

Electrical upgrades involve enhancing or modernizing an existing electrical system to improve its performance, efficiency, or safety. This could include an Electrical Panel Upgrade to accommodate increased power demands, Whole House Rewiring to replace outdated wiring with newer, more energy-efficient materials, or Lighting Additions to upgrade lighting fixtures and enhance aesthetics and functionality.

## FAQs

## Should you hire an electrician for home repairs?

Yes, it is highly recommended to hire a qualified electrician for home repairs involving electrical systems. Attempting DIY electrical repairs can be dangerous and may result in serious injury or property damage. Fortunately, Colonial Electric offers professional electrical repair services to ensure the safety and functionality of your home’s electrical systems.

## How do you know if you need an electrician?

You may need to hire an electrician if you experience any of the following issues: 

- Frequent power outages

- Flickering lights

- Tripping circuit breakers

- Burning smells from outlets or switches

- If you notice exposed wiring.

It’s always best to err on the side of caution and consult with our professionals to assess and address any electrical concerns.

## What are the 3 most common causes of electrical problems?

The three most common causes of electrical problems include: 

Faulty Wiring: Improperly installed or old wiring can lead to electrical problems such as short circuits, power outages, and even electrical fires. Faulty wiring is often a result of poor installation, wear and tear, or damage over time.

Overloaded Circuits: When too many appliances or devices are connected to a single circuit, it can lead to overloading. Overloaded circuits can cause circuit breakers to trip frequently, flickering lights, or even overheating of electrical components, increasing the risk of electrical hazards.

Electrical Surges: Electrical surges are sudden spikes in voltage that can damage electronic devices and appliances connected to the power supply. Surges can be caused by lightning strikes, power grid fluctuations, or faulty wiring within the home. Using surge protectors and ensuring proper grounding can help mitigate the risk of damage from electrical surges.
